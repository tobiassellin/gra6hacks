Guide & Frequently Asked Questions (GTA 6 Cheats FAQ)

How do you enter cheat codes via phone in GTA 6?

You no longer dial phone numbers in GTA 6. Pull out your smartphone by pressing Up on the D-Pad (↑), hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (or hold LB and press RB on Xbox Series X|S), and then enter the directional D-pad sequence. Remember that the code will not be accepted until the cheat's challenge requirement is completed!

Do cheat codes disable saving, Trophies, and Achievements?

Yes. Just like in Red Dead Redemption 2, activating a cheat code in GTA 6 disables manual and auto-saving as well as PlayStation Trophies and Xbox Achievements for the rest of that session. Always create a manual backup save file before enabling cheats.

How do you unlock the Hauners Rhino Tank and P-996 Lazer Jet in GTA 6?

To unlock the Hauners Rhino Tank cheat, beat the main story and complete all Survival Challenges. To unlock the Hauners P-996 Lazer fighter jet cheat, you must complete all challenges in the entire game (100% Challenges completionist reward).

Is there a money cheat code ($250,000) in GTA 6?

Yes! Unlike GTA V, GTA 6 includes a verified money cheat that grants $250,000 cash instantly once you finish the main story. Open your phone (D-Pad Up ↑), hold L1 and press R1 (PS5) / hold LB and press RB (Xbox), and enter: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.
