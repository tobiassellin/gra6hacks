Because regular cheat codes in Grand Theft Auto VI remain locked until you beat all 91 main story missions, Safe Codes are the fastest way to score early cash during your first hours in Chapter 2 (Port Gellhorn). While regular Store Robberies use randomized codes every time, the three Illegal Operations in Port Gellhorn have fixed 4-digit safe codes:

Brooks Boatworks – Safe Code

Code: 6823
Located in northeastern Port Gellhorn. Slip through the hole in the eastern fence and head up to the second-floor office of the gray building. Pro tip: Inspect the map on the wall next to the safe to automatically log all three Illegal Operations safe codes in-game!

La Cascara Motel – Safe Code

Code: 6969
Found in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2). Break into the motel's back office (Illegal Operation) and enter the fixed code 6969 on the keypad to loot the safe for early-game cash.

Kingpin Bowling – Safe Code

Code: 0300
Located inside the back office of the Kingpin Bowling alley in Port Gellhorn. Punch in the fixed combination 0300 to open the safe immediately without hunting for clues.

Regular Store Robberies

Code: Randomized Every Time
Unlike the three Illegal Operations above, regular convenience stores, gas stations, and pawn shops across Leonida use randomly generated safe combinations on each robbery.

How to Enter Cheat Codes in GTA 6 – Step by Step

In our complete database of all 61 GTA 6 cheats, you will find both verified D-Pad directional codes and exact unlock requirements for every vehicle, weapon, and screen filter across Vice City and Leonida.

Follow these four steps to enter directional codes properly on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S:

  1. Beat the main story (91 missions) & the cheat's challenge first: In GTA 6, the Cheats menu (Progress → Cheats) unlocks after finishing the story. Important: The game will not accept a directional D-Pad code until that specific cheat's challenge is completed!
  2. Save your game manually on a backup slot: Activating any cheat immediately disables saving, PlayStation Trophies, and Xbox Achievements for your current play session.

Guide & Frequently Asked Questions (GTA 6 Cheats FAQ)

How do you enter cheat codes via phone in GTA 6?

You no longer dial phone numbers in GTA 6. Pull out your smartphone by pressing Up on the D-Pad (↑), hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (or hold LB and press RB on Xbox Series X|S), and then enter the directional D-pad sequence. Remember that the code will not be accepted until the cheat's challenge requirement is completed!

Do cheat codes disable saving, Trophies, and Achievements?

Yes. Just like in Red Dead Redemption 2, activating a cheat code in GTA 6 disables manual and auto-saving as well as PlayStation Trophies and Xbox Achievements for the rest of that session. Always create a manual backup save file before enabling cheats.

How do you unlock the Hauners Rhino Tank and P-996 Lazer Jet in GTA 6?

To unlock the Hauners Rhino Tank cheat, beat the main story and complete all Survival Challenges. To unlock the Hauners P-996 Lazer fighter jet cheat, you must complete all challenges in the entire game (100% Challenges completionist reward).

Is there a money cheat code ($250,000) in GTA 6?

Yes! Unlike GTA V, GTA 6 includes a verified money cheat that grants $250,000 cash instantly once you finish the main story. Open your phone (D-Pad Up ↑), hold L1 and press R1 (PS5) / hold LB and press RB (Xbox), and enter: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.
