How do you activate cheat codes via controller or phone?

In modern Rockstar games, there are two ways to trigger cheats. The fastest method is pressing a classic button combo directly on your PlayStation 5 DualSense or Xbox Series X|S controller during normal gameplay (outside the pause menu). Press the buttons in a steady rhythm and a confirmation message will appear above the radar. Alternatively, open your character's in-game cell phone, bring up the keypad, and dial the specific cheat number—which is often easier since you don't have to worry about button timing.
