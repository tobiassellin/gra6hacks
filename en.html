Whether you want to unleash total mayhem on the neon-lit streets of Vice City, spawn a supercar in Leonida, or ditch a 5-star police chase, you can find every combination right here in our searchable cheat code list.

Follow these four simple steps to enter cheats safely on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S without losing your Story Mode progress:

  1. Beat the main story & unlock the challenge: In GTA 6, the Cheats menu (61 cheats total under Progress → Cheats) unlocks after completing all 91 main missions plus each cheat's challenge.
  2. Save your game manually first: Activating any cheat immediately disables saving, Trophies, and Achievements for your current play session.
  3. Open your phone and hold L1+R1 / LB+RB: Press Up on the D-Pad to pull out your in-game smartphone, then hold L1 and press R1 (PS5) or LB and press RB (Xbox).

Guide & Frequently Asked Questions (GTA 6 Cheats FAQ)

How do you enter cheat codes via phone in GTA 6?

Rockstar has overhauled the system in GTA 6: you no longer dial phone numbers. Instead, pull out your character's smartphone by pressing Up on the D-Pad, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (or hold LB and press RB on Xbox Series X|S), and then enter the directional D-pad combination. You can also toggle unlocked cheats in the pause menu under Progress → Cheats.

Do cheat codes disable saving, Trophies, and Achievements?

Yes. Just like in Red Dead Redemption 2, activating a cheat code in GTA 6 disables manual and auto-saving as well as PlayStation Trophies and Xbox Achievements for the rest of that session. Always create a manual backup save file before enabling cheats.

Why do cheats have unlock requirements in GTA 6?

GTA 6 features 61 total cheats (10 Player, 11 Combat, 21 Spawn Vehicle, 3 Environment, and 16 Screen Filters). To unlock them, you must first complete all 91 main story missions and then complete each cheat's specific open-world challenge—such as winning shooting range medals, completing 100 workouts, or landing 50 stunt jumps.

Is there a money cheat code ($250,000) in GTA 6?

Yes! Unlike GTA V, GTA 6 includes a verified money cheat that grants $250,000 cash instantly once you finish the main story. Open your phone (D-Pad Up), hold L1/LB + press R1/RB, and enter: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.
