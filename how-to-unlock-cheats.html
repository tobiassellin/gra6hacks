3 Reasons Your GTA 6 Cheat Code Is Not Activating

If you enter a verified D-Pad code and no confirmation banner appears on screen, check these three common mistakes first:

  1. You haven't finished the 91 main story missions yet: In GTA 6, every cheat code is globally locked during your first playthrough of the campaign. The Progress → Cheats menu only unlocks after completing the final main mission. (If you are still in Chapters 1–5, use our 46 Fixed Safe & Door Codes for fast cash and free weapons instead!)

  3. You didn't open the phone and hold L1+R1 / LB+RB first: You cannot enter D-Pad codes during normal movement like in GTA V, and you no longer dial phone numbers. You must first open your smartphone with D-Pad Up (↑), hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (or hold LB and press RB on Xbox Series X|S), and then input the directional sequence.

Step-by-Step: How to Enter Cheats Properly on PS5 & Xbox Series X|S

  1. Create a manual backup save first: As soon as any cheat activates, saving your game and earning PlayStation Trophies or Xbox Achievements are disabled for the rest of that session.
