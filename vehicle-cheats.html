How Vehicle Spawn Cheats Work in GTA 6 (`Left, Up, Right, Down` Prefix)

In Grand Theft Auto VI, every vehicle spawn directional code shares the same 4-button starting prefix: Left, Up, Right, Down. To spawn a vehicle via your smartphone:

  2. Press D-Pad Up (↑) to pull out your in-game smartphone.
  3. On PS5, hold L1 and press R1 (on Xbox Series X|S, hold LB and press RB).
