Pelican Valley Walkthrough: How to Get the Free Crabshack VF5 SMG

One of the best early-game secrets in Key Lento is the unique Crabshack VF5 SMG—a custom military-grade submachine gun manufactured by Vom Feuer that permanently unlocks in your weapon locker once collected at the Pelican Valley Crab Research Outpost.

  1. Flip the outdoor power breaker: The lab computer inside the main building has no power when you first arrive. Walk around the exterior wall and flip the utility power switch.
  2. Log into the terminal with CLEAVES: Look at the floor of the adjoining side room to spot the word CLEAVES scratched into the boards. Enter CLEAVES on the PC to read an email stating the office safe combination is double the specimen number 0036.
  3. Open the office safe with 0072: Doubling 0036 gives 0072. Loot the cash inside and grab the dock security memo.
  4. Unlock the dock building with 0212: Head down to the locked boathouse at the water's edge and enter 0212 on the keypad. Inside you will find the Crabshack VF5 SMG, cash bundles, and a jet ski for a clean getaway.

Chapter 2: Port Gellhorn Illegal Operations (6823, 6969, 0300)

Once the story moves to Port Gellhorn in Chapter 2, three static Illegal Operations become available right away:

  • Brooks Boatworks (6823): Located in northeastern Port Gellhorn. Sneak through the gap in the eastern perimeter fence and climb the stairs to the second-floor office of the gray building.
  • La Cascara Motel (6969): Enter the motel's rear manager office and punch 6969 into the safe.
  • Kingpin Bowling (0300): Head into the back office behind the lanes at Kingpin Bowling and enter 0300.
Pro Tip – Auto-Log All 3 Port Gellhorn Safes: Inspect the wall map hanging right next to the safe upstairs at Brooks Boatworks. Examining that map automatically marks all three Illegal Operations and their combinations in your in-game journal!

Chapter 3: Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs & Unlocking the Snap Gun

In Chapter 3, the fence Yasmani tasks you with three high-value jewelry store and pawn shop heists. Completing this storyline also rewards you with the Snap Gun, allowing you to mechanically pop standard locked back doors during future robberies:

  • Casa de Empeño (1680): Opens the vault holding the Buffalo Skull Chain.
  • Richardson Jewelry & Diamonds (6815): Opens the safe containing the Diamond Ring.
  • Donovan Banks & Co. (0104): Opens the safe holding the Amethyst Necklace (remember the leading zero!).

Vice City & La Perle Secrets: Unique Weapons & Cars (Black Eagle, Janjak's & Boobie's Garage)

Across Vice City (Balsero & La Perle) and Belville (Ambrosia), four newly verified fixed keypad and safe codes grant permanent unique weapons and custom vehicles completely free:

  • Black Eagle Warehouse – Balsero (789987 & 031462): Enter the 6-digit code 789987 on the back office door keypad, then crack the interior office safe with 031462 to claim the unique Black Eagle Import Kalinga .50 pistol.
  • La Perle Garage – Auntie's Place (92665) [MISSABLE!]: Log into the Chapter 3 safehouse bedroom laptop using Muffins → Queen → 15, then enter 92665 into the roller-door keypad on Del Rio Boulevard to claim the garage and Vanessa's custom pink Pfister Growler (must be completed before finishing Chapter 3!).

  • Abandoned Laundry – Northwest Belville (62412): Just south of the Biker Hideout, drop through the hole in the roof to find a reinforced wall safe (clue hidden underneath a collectible card).

Watson Bay (La Polilla Negra) & Haunted Marbelle Hotel Secrets

Two of the most rewarding gang operations in Story Mode are held by La Polilla Negra around Watson Bay & Bobcat Key, while southern Crosstown in Vice City hides the game's famous supernatural Easter egg safe:

  • Watson Bay Safehouse (1819): Northeast of Watson Bay in Grassrivers. Found via the kitchen computer email; rewards cash beneath the microwave, the suppressed black-and-gold La Polilla Negra VF-G1 rifle upstairs, a luxury watch, and an untracked Canis Black Moth Kamacho truck in the garage.
  • Palmer Fishing Warehouse – Bobcat Key (1887): Located on Bobcat Key in Watson Bay (also reported as 1886 on select patch builds); yields $3,400–$5,400+ in cash, a luxury watch, and the rare golden La Polilla Negra KZ-59 assault rifle.
  • Marbelle Hotel – Southern Crosstown (955214): The haunted hotel featuring a Silent Hill Easter egg displays the 6-digit combination 955214 on the top-floor wall inscription to unlock the ground-floor safe.
  • Biker Hideout (1982) & Leonida Carrier Warehouse (9256): The Final Chapter MC clubhouse at The Thirsty Wolf in Belville uses the gang's founding year (1982), while Leonida Carrier Warehouse's back office in Balsero is entered via the roof hatch and unlocked with 9256.

How to Crack Randomized Store Robbery Safes (Chapter 3+)

If you rob a regular convenience store, gas station, or dynamic gang stash where no preset code works, the safe uses a randomized combination generated for that specific robbery. Use any of these three methods to open it:

  1. Activate Focus Mode (L3 + R3): Click both thumbsticks to highlight nearby clues such as sticky notes under desks, wall calendars, ledgers, or framed photos hiding the 4-digit code.
  2. Intimidate the Store Clerk or Guard: Aim your weapon at the manager or cashier and use the Threaten / Safe Code prompt before police arrive.
  3. Plant a Sticky Bomb: If the clerk has fled, slap a Sticky Bomb onto the safe door to blow it open immediately (this triggers a loud alarm and raises your Wanted Level).
